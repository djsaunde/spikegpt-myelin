## 6. Recommendations (loss terms for undesirable states)

`myelin.losses.spike_rate_loss` / `SpikeRateLoss` exist but are **not** wired into language-model training, and they target the **global mean** firing rate — they cannot fix per-neuron dead/saturation (a population can hit a 10% mean with half its neurons dead and half saturated). The targeted fix is a **per-neuron homeostatic** penalty `((spikes.mean(dim=(0,1)) - target)**2).mean()` that pushes each channel's own rate toward a target, optionally with an EMA for a stabler estimate. Deferred until the analysis shows it is needed.
