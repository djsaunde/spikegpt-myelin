# SpikeGPT spike dynamics over training (ctx-3072 run)

Companion to the per-checkpoint analysis (`spikegpt_spike_analysis_*.md`,
generated by `examples/analyze_spikegpt_spikes.py`). Two data sources, with
different resolution.

## A. Density over training (wandb scalars — full trajectory, coarse)

`train/embedding_spike_rate` and `train/mean_block_spike_rate` (mean over all 24
block LIF populations, embedding excluded) were logged every eval step. They show
spike density **rising then plateauing** — the network learns to fire *more* early
in training and settles by ~step 80k:

| step | emb spike rate | mean block spike rate |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 5,000 | 51.4% | 10.8% |
| 20,000 | 54.9% | 14.1% |
| 40,000 | 61.9% | 16.0% |
| 60,000 | 64.2% | 16.7% |
| 80,000 | 65.2% | 17.3% |
| 100,000 | 65.2% | 17.3% |
| 120,000 | 65.4% | 17.4% |
| 139,000 | 65.4% | 17.6% |

Limitation: these are only two global means — no per-layer, per-position, or
dead/saturated breakdown over time.

## B. Deep snapshots (3 retained checkpoints — full breakdown, sparse)

Full analysis run on the only retained ctx-3072 checkpoints (`--max-windows 64` of
`data/enwik8_test`):

| checkpoint | overall density | dead block-neurons | saturated | block0.time | block11.channel |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| step ~70k (`step70k.bak`) | 20.10% | 19 | 0 | 9.6% | 52.0% |
| step ~80k (`resumed.best`) | 20.26% | 18 | 0 | 9.7% | 52.2% |
| step 139k (`final139k`) | 20.45% | 15 | 0 | 10.4% | 51.2% |

(Overall density includes the dense ~65% embedding population; the block-only mean
matches the wandb `mean_block_spike_rate` above.)

## Takeaways

- **Stable, healthy dynamics.** Overall density barely moves over the last ~half of
  training (20.1% → 20.5%); the depth-increasing firing profile (block 0 ~10% →
  block 11 ~51%, channel/lif2 > time/lif1) is established and stable.
- **No degenerate states forming.** Dead block-neurons *decrease* over training
  (19 → 15 of 12,288 block neurons, ~0.1%) and **zero** saturate. The handful of
  dead channels recover rather than accumulate — no regularizer is needed for this
  run.
- **Limitation:** the full per-layer/per-position/dead-saturated breakdown exists at
  only 3 training steps (the retained checkpoints); finer trends rely on the two
  coarse wandb scalars. Future runs could log per-population density + dead-fraction
  at eval cadence via `collect_spike_statistics` for a continuous record.
